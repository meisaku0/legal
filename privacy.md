# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 18, 2026 | **Last Updated:** November 23, 2026

Welcome to Meisaku ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and keeping the platform secure and creator-friendly when you use our website (https://meisaku.art), services, and community features (collectively, the "Services").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information, with a strong emphasis on artist rights. By accessing or using our Services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide to Us
* **Account Credentials:** When you register, we collect your email address, username, and password.
* **Social Logins:** If you choose to register or log in using a third-party social media account (e.g., Google, Discord, X), we receive and store your unique User ID from that platform solely to authenticate your account for future logins. We do not pull or store your contacts or post on your behalf.
* **Optional Age Information:** We do not currently require a date of birth during registration. If you choose to provide one later in your profile or to another age-related feature, we will use it only for age enforcement and applicable legal compliance. You may choose not to provide this information unless it is separately required for a feature that cannot operate without it.

### 1.2 User-Generated Content and Metadata
* **Artworks and Media:** We collect the images and content you upload.
* **Privacy by Design (EXIF Stripping):** To protect your privacy and physical location, our system automatically strips and deletes all EXIF data and hidden metadata (such as GPS coordinates and software details) from your image files upon upload. The files stored on our servers are completely clean.

### 1.3 Activity and Event Analytics
We collect limited event data about interactions you make while using the Services, such as page or feature use, artwork-related actions, account actions, navigation events, and interactions with platform controls. These events help us understand how the Services are used, improve features, diagnose technical issues, evaluate performance, and prevent abuse.

We may associate event data with a user ID, session identifier, or device or browser identifier when needed to provide and improve the Services or understand usage. We do not use this event data to track you across unrelated websites or services.

### 1.4 Payment Information
If you make purchases, buy premium subscriptions, or engage in artist commissions, your payments are processed entirely through secure third-party payment gateways (such as Stripe or PayPal). **We do not collect or store your full credit card numbers or financial details on our servers.**

## 2. How We Use Your Information

### 2.1 Providing and Improving the Platform
We use your information to manage your account, host your portfolios, and provide access to the platform.

### 2.2 Privacy-Focused Analytics
We use privacy-focused analytics tools (similar to Umami) to understand website traffic, platform usage, feature interactions, and performance trends. We collect event data only for legitimate product and security purposes, such as debugging, measuring feature use, improving the Services, and preventing abuse.

These tools do not use invasive third-party tracking cookies or sell your event data. We do not use event analytics to track your activity across unrelated websites or services.

### 2.3 Strict "No AI Training" Guarantee
We respect artists' rights. **We do not use, and will never use, any user-uploaded artwork, text, or content to train artificial intelligence (AI) models, machine learning algorithms, or generative image systems.** Furthermore, we do not sell or license your content to third parties for the purpose of AI training.

## 3. Communication and Moderation

* **Public Interactions:** Your profile, artworks, and public comments are visible to the community.
* **Private Messaging (Direct Messages):** We offer private messaging between users. These messages are strictly confidential. However, to keep the environment safe, our administration and moderation team reserves the right to access and read specific message threads **only** if a participant explicitly reports the conversation for harassment, spam, or Terms of Service violations.

## 4. International Data Transfers and Storage

Meisaku operates globally, and our primary servers and secure object storage infrastructure are located in **Japan**. By using our Services, you understand and agree that your personal information and uploaded content will be transferred to, stored, and processed in Japan. We use secure environments to isolate and protect your data and handle it in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable international data protection standards.

## 5. Adults-Only Service

* **Minimum Age:** You must be at least **18 years old** to access, register for, or use any part of the Services, including viewing, searching, uploading, and messaging.
* **Age Assurance:** At present, access is based on account registration and the user's declaration that they are at least 18. We do not currently require date-of-birth verification at registration. If we introduce age-verification tools or you voluntarily provide age information, we will describe the applicable processing in this Policy. If we reasonably believe that an account belongs to someone under 18, we may suspend or terminate it and delete associated data and content, subject to applicable law.
* **Global Access:** The Services are offered globally, but availability and processing may vary based on the laws that apply where you are located. You are responsible for using the Services only where lawful.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., GDPR in Europe, CCPA in California), you have the right to:
* **Access** the personal data we hold about you.
* **Request deletion** of your account or personal data.
* **Correct** inaccurate information in your profile.
* **Unlink** third-party social media accounts from your profile at any time.

The platform does not currently provide automatic account deletion or a bulk export of personal data. To request the correction or removal of specific information, or to ask about the availability of account deletion, please contact us at support@meisaku.art. We will process requests subject to applicable law and technical limitations.

We may retain limited information where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or enforcement of these Terms. We may also need to preserve certain records even after account deletion.

## 7. Data Security

We implement strong technical measures, including isolated deployments and encrypted storage pipelines, to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. While no system is perfectly secure, we continuously monitor our infrastructure to safeguard the Meisaku community.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect changes in legal requirements or platform features. Material changes will be communicated via platform notifications or email.
